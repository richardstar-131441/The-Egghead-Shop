About The Egghead Shop

A student e-commerce business project serving customers in Kampala.

Who We Are

The Egghead Shop is a student business project based in Nakawa, Kampala, Uganda. The website allows customers to view the listed egg product, see the displayed price and contact the business to ask about orders.

Our intended customers include wholesalers, small supermarkets, bakeries and other customers who buy eggs.

Our Value Proposition

We make it easier for customers in Kampala to find the displayed egg price, contact the shop through WhatsApp and arrange delivery after confirming the order details.

Where We Serve

Our stated location is Nakawa, Kampala. Delivery may be arranged within Kampala. Delivery charges and timing should be confirmed with the shop before an order is accepted.
