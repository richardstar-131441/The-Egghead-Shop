How to Place an Order

  1. Contact The Egghead Shop through WhatsApp or email.
  2. Tell us the number of trays you would like.
  3. Ask us to confirm availability and the displayed price.
  4. Confirm your delivery location and any delivery charge before accepting the order.
  5. Agree on the order details with the shop. No online payment is enabled for this student project.
