CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Policies and Customer Terms

These simple policies explain how enquiries and proposed orders are handled for this student project.

1. Ordering Policy

Customers should contact the shop to confirm product availability, quantity, price and delivery arrangements. An enquiry does not mean an order has been accepted.

2. Delivery Policy

Delivery may be arranged within Kampala. The delivery fee, delivery location and expected timing must be confirmed with the shop before dispatch. No fixed delivery fee or delivery time is promised on this website.

3. Product Concerns and Refund Enquiries

If an order has a problem, contact the shop promptly and explain the issue. The details should be discussed directly so that an appropriate response can be agreed. This student website does not process refunds or payments.

4. Privacy Notice

For this student demonstration, customers should not submit sensitive personal information, payment details, passwords or identity documents through the website. Contact details shared in an enquiry should only be used to respond to that enquiry and discuss the requested order. Do not send personal information that is not needed.

This is a coursework privacy notice, not a substitute for a full legal privacy assessment under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

5. Website and Payment Disclaimer

This website is a student project for BUC3106 at MUBS. It is a demonstration of an online business presence. Payments are not active, and no payment should be made through sample details shown on the website.

6. Accuracy of Information

The displayed selling price is UGX 14,000 per tray. Customers should confirm current price and availability before ordering. Delivery fees and timing are not fixed on this website and must be agreed directly.
